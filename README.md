# TherapyTrack

Comprehensive mental health therapy session management platform ensuring secure patient care coordination on the Stacks blockchain.

## Overview

TherapyTrack facilitates secure management of mental health therapy sessions, patient progress tracking, and therapist-patient coordination while maintaining strict privacy and confidentiality standards.

## Features

- **Therapist Verification**: Licensed therapist registration and verification system
- **Patient Management**: Secure patient registration with therapy type matching
- **Session Scheduling**: Flexible therapy session scheduling and management
- **Progress Tracking**: Comprehensive therapy goals and progress monitoring
- **Outcome Recording**: Detailed session outcomes and treatment adjustment tracking
- **Mood Monitoring**: Pre and post-session mood tracking for treatment effectiveness

## Smart Contract Functions

### Public Functions

- `register-therapist`: Verify and register licensed mental health professionals
- `register-patient`: Enroll patients with appropriate therapist matching
- `schedule-session`: Create therapy session appointments with flexible scheduling
- `complete-session`: Record session completion with mood and homework tracking
- `record-session-outcome`: Document session outcomes and treatment progress
- `create-therapy-goal`: Establish therapy goals with target dates and tracking
- `update-goal-progress`: Monitor and update therapy goal achievement progress

### Read-Only Functions

- `get-patient-record`: Access patient registration and treatment history
- `get-therapist-profile`: View therapist credentials and current caseload
- `get-therapy-session`: Retrieve detailed therapy session information
- `get-session-outcome`: Access session outcome and progress documentation
- `get-therapy-goal`: View therapy goals and current progress status
- `get-next-session-id`: Get the next available session identifier
- `get-next-goal-id`: Get the next available goal identifier

## Usage

Deploy the contract to establish a secure mental health platform, register verified therapists, and begin coordinating patient care with comprehensive progress tracking.
